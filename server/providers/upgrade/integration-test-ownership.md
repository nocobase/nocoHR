# Fixed-template test ownership

The 43 test/fixture paths in [the full ledger](template-ownership.tsv) are reconciled by the application consumer they verify. Test files are not copied over business fixtures merely to match upstream filenames. Existing same-name application tests participate in the full application run; its failures remain failures in the acceptance matrix. This table explains the twelve upstream filenames not present in NocoHR.

The other 31 test/fixture paths exist in this repository. They keep their application expectations rather than receiving a blanket upstream overwrite. The current full-run log, the [acceptance matrix](integration-acceptance.md), and the independently reviewed F1–F4 unit records identify their outcomes and scopes. This ownership decision does not turn an unexecuted upstream assertion, a known failing fixture or a missing production deployment into a passing check.
